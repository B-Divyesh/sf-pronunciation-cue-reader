Plain-language policy · August 30, 2026

Privacy

Your reading material and pronunciation cues stay in your browser.

What the extension stores

The extension stores pronunciation cues and their site scope in local extension storage. A context-menu selection is stored for up to ten minutes only to hand it to the popup. It is deleted when used, expired, malformed, or dated in the future.

What is sent

Say It Right does not send selected text or pronunciation cues to us. Reading uses speech voices already installed in or exposed by your browser.

Exports and deletion

Glossary export happens only when you request it. The JSON file stays where you save it. Delete individual cues, or remove the extension and its browser data to delete all stored data.

Website

This site has no advertising, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or behavioral analytics. The host may retain standard security logs.

Contact

For privacy questions, email privacy@sociobot.in.
