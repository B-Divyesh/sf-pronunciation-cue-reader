Plain-language policy · August 30, 2026

Privacy

Your selected text and pronunciation cues stay in your browser.

What the extension stores

The extension stores pronunciation cues and their site in local extension storage. Selected text from the context menu is kept for up to ten minutes to hand to the popup.

What is sent

Say It Right does not send selected text or pronunciation cues to us. Reading uses speech voices already installed in or exposed by your browser.

Exports and deletion

Saved-cue export happens only when you request it. The JSON file stays where you save it. Delete individual pronunciation cues, or remove the extension and its browser data to delete all stored data.

Website

This site has no advertising, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or behavioral analytics. The host may retain standard security logs.

Contact

For privacy questions, email privacy@sociobot.in.
